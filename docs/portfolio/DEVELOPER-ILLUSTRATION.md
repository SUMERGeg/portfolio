# Иллюстрация «Как я работаю»

Изображение создано встроенным инструментом `imagegen` для страницы «О себе». Это условный силуэт разработчика, а не фотография Егора. Исходник с прозрачностью: `src/assets/about/developer-silhouette.png`. Astro выпускает адаптивные WebP. Подписи, линии и точки реализованы отдельно в `DeveloperMap.astro`.

## Промпт

Create one premium editorial 3D illustration asset for a Russian web designer and developer portfolio. A calm anonymous male developer silhouette seated behind an open laptop, facing slightly to the viewer's right in a three-quarter view. Composition: full bust, head around x=46% y=24%, face/eye region around x=53% y=29%, both hands clearly resting on keyboard around x=49% y=62%, laptop display centered around x=66% y=58%. Laptop and desk visible, shoulders and arms anatomically coherent. Sculptural matte dark graphite / charcoal material with refined silver rim lighting, small restrained electric blue reflected highlights from the laptop on hands and cheek. Subtle faceless simplified features, no photoreal portrait, no hood, no hacker aesthetics. Very high quality restrained product sculpture, natural proportions, elegant simple silhouettes, soft studio shadows. Full object entirely inside frame with generous transparent margin. Transparent background, no background panels, no floor fill, no white matte. Square composition. No text, no symbols, no diagram, no arrows, no connector lines, no circles, no floating UI, no logos. The result will sit over a near-black #080c12 website, so contours must be visibly defined by soft neutral silver light. Focus on craftsmanship rather than futuristic ornament.
